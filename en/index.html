Timecode Stopwatch

Timecode Stopwatch is a professional timecode stopwatch designed for video creators, animators, and content production workflows.

The application is designed to make time and frame-based measurements easier when working with video, animation, storyboards, and related production tasks.

Features

  • Timecode display for 24, 25, 30, 50, and 60 fps
  • NTSC Drop-Frame Timecode support for 29.97 and 59.94 fps
  • PAL and integer frame-rate support
  • LAP recording
  • Timecode-based time and frame measurement
  • Start and Reset button position switching
  • Japanese and English user interface

Platform: Android

An iOS version is planned for a future release.
